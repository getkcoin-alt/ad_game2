Privacy Policy — Chatpata

App: Chatpata (package in.scriza.chatpata) · Developer: Karnveer Singh, Scriza · Last updated: 16 November 2026

Chatpata is a free cooking puzzle game. We built it to collect as little information as possible. There are no accounts and no sign-in, and we never ask for your name, phone number, contacts, photos, location or messages.

1. What the game stores on your device

Your progress (levels, stars, coins, kitchen decorations, settings and language) is saved only on your phone or browser. Uninstalling the app or clearing site data deletes it.

2. Information collected

You can turn off anonymous gameplay statistics and personalised ads at any time in Settings → Privacy inside the game. You can also reset or delete your Android advertising ID in your phone's Settings → Google → Ads.

3. Advertising

The game shows only rewarded ads that you choose to watch (for example, +5 moves). There are no pop-up or banner ads. Ads are provided by Google. Google may use cookies or the advertising ID to show and measure ads; personalised ads are off by default and only used if you switch them on. Where the law requires it (e.g. EEA, UK and some US states), Google's consent form is shown before ads load. Learn more: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

4. Sharing

When you tap Share, the game creates a picture of your win and hands it to your phone's share menu (e.g. WhatsApp). Nothing is sent unless you choose where to share it. We do not sell your information.

5. Children

Chatpata is designed for adults (18+) and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has used the game and you want data removed, contact us.

6. Data security and retention

Game data stays on your device. Anonymous analytics and ad data held by Google are kept according to Google's own retention policies. All network traffic uses encrypted HTTPS.

7. Your choices and rights

You can switch off analytics and personalised ads in the game, reset your advertising ID, or uninstall the app to delete all local data. To ask a question or request deletion of any data linked to your install ID, email us.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date.
